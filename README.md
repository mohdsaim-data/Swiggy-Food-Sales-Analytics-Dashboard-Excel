# 🛵 Swiggy Food Sales & Data Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This interactive Excel dashboard analyzes Swiggy food ordering and sales data across major geographic, temporal, and product categories. It unifies complex order volumes, revenue metrics, and customer rating distributions into a single executive dashboard powered by Excel PivotTables, interactive slicers, and dynamic charts to support data-driven decision-making for food delivery platforms and restaurant partners.

## Business Problem Solved
Food delivery platforms and restaurant chains face challenges in identifying top-performing geographical markets, optimizing regional menu pricing, and monitoring temporal demand fluctuations. This dashboard addresses these operational challenges by providing:
* **Geographic & City Sales Tracking:** Pinpointing high-performing regions and states (e.g., Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh) to optimize rider distribution, regional marketing campaigns, and restaurant onboarding.
* **Product & Category Mix Optimization:** Analyzing sales across food types (Veg vs. Non-Veg) and price points to guide menu engineering, average order value (AOV) growth strategies, and targeted promotions.
* **Temporal & Demand Trend Monitoring:** Tracking ordering patterns across quarters, months, and days of the week to streamline operational logistics, driver availability, and peak-hour delivery fulfillment.

## Key Insights & Metrics
* **Total Sales Revenue:** ₹53.01 Million generated across total platform orders.
* **Order Volume & Ticket Size:** **197.43K total orders** processed with an Average Order Value (AOV) of **₹268.51**.
* **Customer Ratings & Engagement:** Maintained a strong **4.34 average rating** across a total rating count of **5.59 Million reviews**.
* **Sales by Food Type:** Main course and meal options dominate revenue share, with major category breakdowns highlighting a **70% share (₹37.27M)** vs. **30% share (₹15.74M)** across food categories.
* **Geographic Demographics & Top Cities:** 
  * **Karnataka** leads state sales at **₹5.46 Million**.
  * **Uttar Pradesh** (**₹3.12 Million**), **Telangana** (**₹3.02 Million**), **Maharashtra** (**₹3.02 Million**), and **Delhi** (**₹2.83 Million**) round out the top 5 sales hubs.
* **Temporal Sales Trends:**
  * **Quarterly Breakdown:** Q2 led with **₹19.9M (74.16K orders)**, closely followed by Q1 at **₹19.7M (73.10K orders)**, and Q3 at **₹13.4M (50.17K orders)**.
  * **Daily Demand:** Weekend order spikes are visible, with **Saturday (₹7.78M)** and **Sunday (₹7.66M)** recording the highest daily cumulative sales volume compared to weekdays.

## How to View & Use
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open [Swiggy_Sales_Dashboard.xlsx](Swiggy_Sales_Dashboard.xlsx) in **Microsoft Excel**.
3. Navigate to the **Dashboard** tab.
4. Utilize the interactive slicers on the left sidebar to dynamically filter metrics by State, Month, and Restaurant profile.
